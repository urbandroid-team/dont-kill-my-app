Nothing OS provides a very near-stock Android experience. If you are experiencing issues with background apps being killed, it is likely due to standard Android battery optimization.

To prevent Nothing OS from aggressively killing apps in the background, you can adjust the battery optimization settings for those specific applications.

### 1. Set Battery Usage to "Unrestricted"
This is the most effective way to stop the system from automatically killing an app.
1. Long-press the **app icon** on your home screen or in your app drawer.
2. Tap **App info** (or the "i" icon).
3. Look for **Battery** or **App battery usage**.
4. Change the setting from "Optimized" to **Unrestricted**.

### 2. Check for Additional Permissions
Some apps require specific permissions to stay active. Within the same **App info** menu:
* Ensure **Background data** is enabled under **Mobile data & WiFi** (if the app requires internet access to stay active).
* Check if the app has a specific "Background running" or "Auto-start" toggle within its own in-app settings (common for utility or wearable apps).
