### Auto-launch (Smart Manager)

TCL devices include a **Smart Manager** app that controls which apps are allowed to start automatically and run in the background. This is the most important setting to configure.

* Open *Phone settings* and tap **Smart Manager** (or search for it)

<div class="img-block">
  <figure>
    <img src="/assets/img/tcl/tcl_settings.png">
    <figcaption>1. Open <em>Phone settings</em> and tap <strong>Smart Manager</strong>.</figcaption>
  </figure>
</div>

* In Smart Manager, tap **Auto-launch**

<div class="img-block">
  <figure>
    <img src="/assets/img/tcl/tcl_smart_manager.png">
    <figcaption>2. In Smart Manager, tap <strong>Auto-launch</strong>.</figcaption>
  </figure>
</div>

* Make sure **Automatically manage** is turned **off**, then find your app in the list and enable its toggle.

<div class="img-block">
  <figure>
    <img src="/assets/img/tcl/tcl_auto_launch.png">
    <figcaption>3. Turn off <strong>Automatically manage</strong>, then enable the toggle for your app.</figcaption>
  </figure>
</div>

### Battery Optimization

* Open *Phone settings > Apps*, find your app and tap **App battery usage**

<div class="img-block">
  <figure>
    <img src="/assets/img/tcl/tcl_appinfo.png">
    <figcaption>4. Open your app's info page and tap <strong>App battery usage</strong>.</figcaption>
  </figure>
</div>

* Make sure **Allow background usage** is enabled.

<div class="img-block">
  <figure>
    <img src="/assets/img/tcl/tcl_appbatteryusage.png">
    <figcaption>5. Enable <strong>Allow background usage</strong>.</figcaption>
  </figure>
</div>
