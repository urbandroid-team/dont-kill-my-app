TCL (also known as TCT Mobile) devices use a custom Android skin that includes an aggressive background process management layer called **Smart Manager** (package: `com.tct.onetouchbooster`).

On default settings, background processing may not work reliably and apps running in the background can be killed without notice.

TCL does not provide any APIs or documentation for these extensions. The behavior may vary between device models and firmware versions.
